# Portfolio visual direction

7 November 2026. This pass starts from commit 915bcdb and responds to Daniel's feedback that the consistent card treatment still felt generic.

## What changed

The home page now reads as a personal editorial cover. Daniel's name combines the existing Newsreader italic with Space Grotesk. A compact portrait, a small dc. mark and a blue project showcase establish a stronger identity using the existing palette and fonts.

The showcase lets visitors choose a digital tool, campaign idea or customer research project. Its image, title, question, ownership note and destination update together. Selection requires a click or keyboard action. There is no automatic cycling. The short entrance animation respects reduced motion.

Selected projects and the main collection use open, staggered compositions rather than identical bordered cards. Project names sit above the collection previews. Mobile returns to a single reading column. The superseded home styles were removed from the shared stylesheet.

Mailchimp has a new illustration of its recorded Jenga concept, linking audience, idea, video and social formats. It is explicitly labelled as a new illustration of the proposal structure, with original campaign assets still unpublished. It does not claim to reproduce original artwork or demonstrate campaign impact.

About puts the portrait beside the name on mobile. Contact uses a blue email panel with readable white actions. Navigation, project filters, focus states and disclosures retain their behaviour. Analytical calculations, data and the standalone Route & Margin demonstration are unchanged.

## References

The original websites of [Studio Feixen](https://www.studiofeixen.ch/) and [Malika Favre](https://www.malikafavre.com/) were inspected in the browser. The useful principles were letting the work establish the visual character, changing the scale of images, and avoiding a repeated interface frame. Their artwork, branding and copy were not used.

## Verification

The new home, collection, marketing section, About and Contact were inspected in the permitted browser. Home and collection were reviewed at desktop and mobile dimensions; the home opening and header were additionally checked at 320px. Home was inspected in both themes. Keyboard selection changed each featured project and its destination. Filters and text search produced the expected project counts. The menu and contact copy action worked.

The 320px check exposed a clipped Menu control. The narrow header now wraps the name and keeps the visible controls within the viewport. The redesigned About and Contact layouts had no horizontal document overflow at 390px. No physical device testing or participant study is claimed.

The in app browser sometimes restored an old or inactive development document through its Back control. Reloading restored the URL's category and query after hydration. This pass does not claim a clean end to end Back restoration check in that browser. The collection state logic was not changed.

ESLint, TypeScript and the production build pass. Route & Margin still passes its 1,080 per trip reconciliation checks, plus its ranking, eligibility, crossover and account total checks. Both current presentation stylesheets parse with no duplicate selectors in the same at rule context. A static export check verifies local links, anchors, images and script destinations separately from the browser inspection.

The remaining evidence gap is the unavailable original marketing artwork. Existing methods, source figures and ownership statements remain available with their projects.
