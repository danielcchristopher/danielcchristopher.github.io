The Process

Five stages, thirty six hours, one oven. Nothing is rushed.

36h
01

Mix

Flour, water, salt and our starter, brought together slowly. Nothing else goes in the bowl.

02

Fold

The dough is folded at intervals rather than kneaded hard. Strength builds gently and the crumb stays open.

03

Cold proof (36 hours)

The dough rests in the cold for a day and a half. Flavour deepens, the tang softens and the crust learns to blister.

04

Shape and score

Each loaf is shaped by hand, then scored with a blade so it opens exactly where it should.

05

Bake

Loaves go into the wood fired oven while the heat is still fierce. The crust darkens and the room smells of smoke and toasted grain.
